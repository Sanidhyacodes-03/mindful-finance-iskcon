Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities.

1. Information We Collect

Spendly collects account identifiers (name, email, hashed passwords) and user-submitted financial transactions, categories, budgets, and goals to provide real-time expense analytics.

2. How We Use Data

Your transaction details are processed locally by our AI calculation engine to project 30-day cash flow forecasts, calculate financial health scores, and generate behavioral spending insight alerts.

3. Data Security & Storage

All user passwords are stored using salted Werkzeug cryptographic hashes. Database queries are fully parameterized to prevent SQL injection vulnerabilities.
